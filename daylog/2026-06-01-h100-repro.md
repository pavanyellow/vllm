Re-running the headline result (`2026-05-30-h100.md` E9: block=8192 kills the
chunked-prefill sawtooth) on a fresh H100 80GB to confirm it reproduces. Started
directly from the recommended default config — no baseline pass this time.

## Setup
- Served `Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-FP8` (as `Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B`). `/v1/models` → 200.
- Box: H100 80GB HBM3, vLLM 0.22.0, torch 2.11.0+cu130, CUDA 13.0, py 3.12, driver 580.126.09.
- Launched the README default verbatim (the block=8192 path):
  ```bash
  vllm serve Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-FP8 \
    --served-model-name Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B \
    --max-model-len 8192 --gpu-memory-utilization 0.90 \
    --enable-prefix-caching --enable-chunked-prefill --language-model-only \
    --reasoning-parser qwen3 --default-chat-template-kwargs '{"enable_thinking":false}' \
    --max-num-batched-tokens 8192 --block-size 8192 --max-num-seqs 8
  ```

  CUDA-graph capture 3s. `init engine ... took 267.89 s (compilation 31.69 s)`.

**Reproduced.** The block=8192 flat band lands at ~80–106 ms, monotonic in length,
with the sawtooth gone: every E8 slow-band length (3000/4000/4096/4752/5000, all
~171–179 ms @1056) collapses onto the fast band, and the once-fast aligned points
(3168, 4224) are unchanged. Run-to-run spread is tight (p50 stable within ~1–3 ms;
4500 the noisiest at 96.9 vs 100.2). p90 hugs p50 (≤~10 ms) → real, not jitter.

Absolute level runs ~5–13 ms *below* the 2026-05-30 E9 p50 on the low/mid lengths
(80 vs 88–94 @3000–3500) and converges at the top (105.7 vs 103.3 @5000). Likely
box/driver delta (driver 580.126.09, CUDA 13.0 here) — the shape of the result
(flat band, no sawtooth, monotonic prefill-compute rise) is identical. Headline
E9 conclusion holds: **block=8192 removes the chunked-prefill sawtooth, up to ~2×
TTFT on worst-case non-aligned prompts**, at the documented KV cost (1.39M→269k tok).
