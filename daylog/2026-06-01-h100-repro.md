Re-running the headline result (`2026-05-30-h100.md` E9: block=8192 kills the
chunked-prefill sawtooth) on a fresh H100 80GB to confirm it reproduces. Started
directly from the recommended default config — no baseline pass this time.

- Box: H100 80GB HBM3, vLLM 0.22.0, torch 2.11.0+cu130, CUDA 13.0, py 3.12, driver 580.126.09.

  ```bash

    --reasoning-parser qwen3 --default-chat-template-kwargs '{"enable_thinking":false}' \
    --max-num-batched-tokens 8192 --block-size 8192 --max-num-seqs 8
  ```

  Launched from a neutral cwd (clone dir shadows `vllm._C`); installed wheel.

  33.65 GiB / 162s, torch.compile 31.7s, DeepGEMM warmup 1253 kernels (~3:22),
  CUDA-graph capture 3s. `init engine ... took 267.89 s (compilation 31.69 s)`.
- **KV: 269,165 tokens, max concurrency 32.86× @8192.** Matches E9 (269,165 / 32.9×)
  to the token — block=8192 KV granularity confirmed identical, not a re-derive.

**Reproduced.** The block=8192 flat band lands at ~80–106 ms, monotonic in length,
with the sawtooth gone: every E8 slow-band length (3000/4000/4096/4752/5000, all
~171–179 ms @1056) collapses onto the fast band, and the once-fast aligned points
(3168, 4224) are unchanged. Run-to-run spread is tight (p50 stable within ~1–3 ms;
4500 the noisiest at 96.9 vs 100.2). p90 hugs p50 (≤~10 ms) → real, not jitter.

Absolute level runs ~5–13 ms *below* the 2026-05-30 E9 p50 on the low/mid lengths
(80 vs 88–94 @3000–3500) and converges at the top (105.7 vs 103.3 @5000). Likely
box/driver delta (driver 580.126.09, CUDA 13.0 here) — the shape of the result
(flat band, no sawtooth, monotonic prefill-compute rise) is identical. Headline
E9 conclusion holds: **block=8192 removes the chunked-prefill sawtooth, up to ~2×
TTFT on worst-case non-aligned prompts**, at the documented KV cost (1.39M→269k tok).

## E9-repro-3 — short-prompt floor 10→800
`python ttft_sweep.py --lengths 10,32,64,128,256,384,512,640,768,800 --concurrency 1 --n 12 --warm 4`
(same server/method). Probes the memory-bound floor below the compute-bound rise.

| in | p50 | p90 | min |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 10 | 21.9 | 22.5 | 21.5 |
| 32 | 75.5 | 76.2 | 75.0 |
| 64 | 77.8 | 79.9 | 74.8 |
| 128 | 79.1 | 79.4 | 76.6 |
| 256 | 78.9 | 83.3 | 75.2 |
| 384 | 73.9 | 75.4 | 71.5 |
| 512 | 75.2 | 76.5 | 73.4 |
| 640 | 75.6 | 79.2 | 71.5 |
| 768 | 76.1 | 77.0 | 74.7 |
| 800 | 75.6 | 76.3 | 74.7 |

Two regimes:
- **L=32→800: flat ~74–79 ms plateau, L-independent.** This is the memory-bound
  prefill floor — TTFT here is the one-time ~32 GB FP8 MoE weight read + fixed
  overhead, not token compute (adding 768 tokens costs ~0 ms). It joins continuously
  to the compute-bound rise measured above: floor ~75 → 95 ms @4200 (E10 wall) →
  153 ms @8100. The compute term only becomes visible above ~1k tokens.
- **L=10 = 21.9 ms — sub-floor.** Falls ~53 ms *below* the plateau because 10 tokens
  × top-8 ≈ 80 expert activations only touch ~70 of 256 experts (coupon-collector
  on 256), so most expert weights are never read this prefill → the dominant HBM
  cost is skipped. Consistent with E6's "all 256 experts hit at L=4096"; the full
  32 GB read (and thus the full floor) only materializes once L·8 ≳ 256, i.e. by
  L≈32 here. So the floor is set by *expert coverage*, not a fixed launch cost.

Whole-curve picture (block=8192, C=1): 22 ms @L=10 → ~75 ms floor (32–800) →
slow compute-bound rise → 153 ms @8100, one clean monotonic line, no sawtooth.
