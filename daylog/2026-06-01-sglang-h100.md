## E-SG9 — warm / cached re-serve (4k, last-N changed): 0% reuse ❌
`kv_suffix.py --url …:30000 --len 4000 --changed 0,10,20,50,100,200,500`. full-cold = 83.2 ms.

| changed N | 0 | 10 | 20 | 50 | 100 | 200 | 500 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| TTFT p50 ms | 83.7 | 83.6 | 83.3 | 83.5 | 83.4 | 83.4 | 83.5 |

**No speedup — warm ≈ cold for every N.** Verified in the scheduler log: every prefill batch (incl. an
identical 2000-tok prompt sent 3×) shows **`#cached-token: 0`**. SGLang's radix cache is **not reusing
anything** for this hybrid GDN model. `prompt_tokens_details: None` too. `page_size=1` only caches the
attention KV — the **mamba/GDN state is the bottleneck**, and SGLang 0.5.12 doesn't checkpoint it for
radix reuse here (despite `--mamba-track-interval 256` being set). Not chased further today.

## The engine inversion (the headline)
| | cold 3k–5k | warm re-serve (4k, ≤500 changed) |
|---|---|---|
| **vLLM** (block=1056 + graphs≤1056 + align mamba cache) | flat ~95–109 ms (**needs graphs+block tuning**) | **~48 ms, 79% reuse** ✅ |
| **SGLang** (page_size=1, radix on, default) | **flat ~80–102 ms, no tuning** ✅ | **~83 ms, 0% reuse** ❌ |

Opposite of the expectation. The GDN mamba state is the universal blocker for hybrid prefix caching;
**vLLM checkpoints it (`align` mode) → warm reuses; SGLang 0.5.12 doesn't → warm = cold.** So SGLang wins
the *cold* path structurally (flat, no sawtooth, no tuning), vLLM wins the *warm/re-serve* path. Neither
gives free warm re-serve out of the box: vLLM needs graphs+align, SGLang needs a mamba-radix fix.
