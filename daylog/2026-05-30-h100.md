## Method (how we work here)
- **Daylog discipline.** Notes stay in this style; add + commit + push after
  *every* experiment (E1, E2, …), not batched at the end.
- **Empirics, not claims.** Record measured numbers with the command and the
  conditions that produced them. No unsubstantiated assertions.
- **Understand before fixing.** Characterize and measure a phenomenon before
  reaching for a fix — work like a scientist.
- **Separate knowledge from guesses.** State plainly what is certain; *measure*
  the parts that would otherwise be a guess.
