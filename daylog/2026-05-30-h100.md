## E8 — H100 baseline: TTFT sweep 3k–5k, block=1056, C=1
Scope narrowed to the 3k–5k path (the prompts we actually serve).
`ttft_sweep.py --lengths 3000,3168,3500,4000,4096,4224,4500,4752,5000
 --concurrency 1 --n 12 --warm 4` (8 samples/len, fresh random ids, cache_frac=0).

| in_len | p50 | p90 | min | chunks (×1056) | tier |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| 3000 | 173.0 | 175.6 | 169.7 | 2112+888 | slow |
| 3168 | 93.9 | 95.2 | 92.9 | 3×1056 | **aligned/fast** |
| 3500 | 114.5 | 116.9 | 112.6 | 3168+332 | mid |
| 4000 | 171.2 | 188.0 | 168.8 | 3168+832 | slow |
| 4096 | 173.0 | 175.3 | 170.7 | 3168+928 | slow |
| 4224 | 99.5 | 105.3 | 96.5 | 4×1056 | **aligned/fast** |
| 4500 | 117.5 | 124.9 | 114.6 | 4224+276 | mid |
| 4752 | 179.4 | 202.5 | 166.0 | 4224+528 | slow |
| 5000 | 174.8 | 181.3 | 169.6 | 4224+776 | slow |

- Sawtooth reproduced on H100. 4096 (173.0) ≈ 1.74× 4224 (99.5) — the
  chunk-split penalty. p90 tight → real, not noise.
- The only fast points in 3k–5k are the two exact multiples of 1056 (3168, 4224).
- OPEN: 2-chunk (non-aligned) cases split into two tiers by remainder size —
  small remainder 276/332 → ~115 ms; large 528–928 → ~171–179 ms. Threshold
  between 332 and 528 (≈ ½ block?). Not yet explained; flag, don't hand-wave.
